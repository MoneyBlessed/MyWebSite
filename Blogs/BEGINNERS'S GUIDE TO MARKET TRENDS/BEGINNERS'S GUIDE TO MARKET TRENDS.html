📈 The MoneyBlessed Beginner’s Guide to Market Trends


— Learn it once, and you’ll never trade blind again.


Why Trends Are Everything


Most beginners lose money not because they can’t analyse, but because they fight the market.

At Money Blessed, we teach one golden rule:


> “You can’t control the waves — but you can learn to surf them.”




Trends are those waves. They tell you which way the money is flowing.

Miss them, and you’re swimming against the tide.

Catch them, and your trades feel effortless.



---


1️⃣ Uptrend (Bullish Trend)


What it is:

Prices make higher highs and higher lows.

Think of a staircase going up — each step is higher than the last.



How to deal with it (Money Blessed way):


Don’t short here — rookie mistake.


Ride the wave with buy-on-dips strategy.


Use a trailing stop-loss to lock profits.


---


2️⃣ Downtrend (Bearish Trend)


What it is:

Prices make lower highs and lower lows.

A staircase going down — each step lower than the last.




How to deal with it (Money Blessed way):


Avoid buying the dip blindly — that works in uptrends, not here.


Either stay out or take short positions if trained.


Protect your portfolio by moving into cash or defensive assets.




3️⃣ Sideways / Range-Bound Trend


What it is:

Price moves between two levels — support (floor) and resistance (ceiling).

No clear up or down — just consolidation.



Trade short-term: Buy near support, sell near resistance.


Avoid big positional trades unless breakout happens.


Watch for volume — breakouts usually come with a spike.
