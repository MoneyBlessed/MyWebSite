Vodafone Idea clarifies on Media report After Shares Tumble 9 percent to RS.6.79 on NSE

On 26 Aug, 2025 Vodafone Idea Limited declares drop of 9 percent in stock price after some news intensed minds of investors. The company's shares fall 9 percent to trade at RS 6.79 on the National Stock Exchange (NSE).In the statement to stock exchanges . The company stated that the company hadn't received any information from the Government about this matter.

The main reason for the fall of stock is that Government stated that no relief is being considered for the telecom operator regarding its AGR dues. Vodafone Idea has been already in a drastic debt and also had to compete with JIO and BHARATI AIRTEL, and also experiencing financial losses.

After this incident, the investors are in seeking support from Government for the company, so that they can solve the issue as it can affect company financially and its performance in stocks.
